PROMPT — "ANT: the life cycle"
(animated short, 90.7 s, 1080x1920 portrait, 30 fps, sampled orchestra + real recordings)

This file holds (1) the full production prompt that describes the finished video, so it can be recreated, and
(2) the requests that were used to arrive at it. The chat that produced this film was not saved on this machine,
so Part 1 was reconstructed from the code in this folder.

====================================================================================================
PART 1 — THE PROMPT
====================================================================================================

Make a portrait (Instagram Reel) motion-design film about the life cycle of an ant colony: the nuptial flight, a
queen founding a new nest, the four stages egg -> larva -> pupa -> adult, the colony as a busy city, and new
queens leaving to start the cycle again. Every frame is drawn in code with the cicada-motion engine; the camera
never stops moving. Original orchestral score plus real CC0 sound recordings, locked to the picture by one cue sheet.

FORMAT
- 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, 90.67 s = 34 bars at 90 BPM (1 bar = 2.667 s); every scene starts on a bar line.
- js/cues.js is the single clock for picture AND music.

STORY / SHOT LIST (bars, from js/cues.js)
- 0–4    flight: a summer evening after rain; winged queens and males pour out and rise; title.
- 4–7    found: a mated queen lands, stores sperm for life, snaps off her wings and digs a sealed chamber.
- 7–11   egg (stage 1): she lays eggs; inside an egg the embryo forms; fertilised = female, unfertilised = male.
- 11–16  larva (stage 2): hatching, fed mouth-to-mouth, four moults, a look inside; diet decides worker or queen.
- 16–21  pupa (stage 3): a silk cocoon; the body dissolves and rebuilds; colour comes in.
- 21–25  adult (stage 4): sisters cut her free; a pale callow darkens; full anatomy.
- 25–30  colony: the nest city — chambers, castes, jobs by age, a pheromone trail, the population grows.
- 30–34  cycle: years later new winged queens leave; the life-cycle ring and lifespans; end card.

MUSIC AND SOUND (js/music/)
- 90 BPM, E minor -> G major. Real CC0 orchestra (VSCO-2 CE samples: strings sustain/tremolo/pizzicato/spiccato,
  harp, flute, clarinet, bassoon, horns, glockenspiel, marimba, xylophone, piano, timpani, cymbals, gong, bass drum,
  hand drums, log drums) plays an original main theme ("Colony") with one musical world per scene: airy harp and
  flute for the flight, log drums for the dig, music box for the egg, marimba for the larva, tremolo mystery for
  the pupa, heroic horns for the adult, driving spiccato for the colony, the grand theme ending in G major.
- Real CC0 recordings (BigSoundBank) for ambience and foley, plus synthesized hum, whooshes, risers, pops and
  cracks. Convolution hall, loudness ridden along the film, -15 LUFS, true-peak limited.

HOW TO REBUILD
- Samples are shared with ../cicada-motion/assets (assets/vsco and assets/sfx here link to them): fetch the
  VSCO-2 CE samples with ../frog-motion/tools/fetch_vsco.js and the recordings with ../cicada-motion/tools/fetch_sfx.sh.
- Score:   node ant-motion/js/music/index.js [out.wav]
- Film:    node ant-motion/tools/render.js --video [--jobs 12] [--music]   -> ant-life-cycle.mp4
- Checks:  node ant-motion/tools/render.js --at 12.5   |   --times 1,5.5,20 --cols 3 --cell 360
