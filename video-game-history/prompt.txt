PROMPT — "HISTORY OF VIDEO GAMES"
(animated short, 128 s, 1080x1080, 60 fps, sampled orchestra + 8-bit chip voices)

This file holds (1) the full production prompt that describes the finished video, so it can be recreated, and
(2) the requests that were used to arrive at it. The chat that produced this film was not saved on this machine,
so Part 1 was reconstructed from the code in this folder.

====================================================================================================
PART 1 — THE PROMPT
====================================================================================================

Make a 128-second motion-design history of video games, drawn entirely in code, from 1958 oscilloscope tennis to
today's esports arenas. Draw each era in its own visual language (oscilloscope dots, vector lines, a wood-grain TV,
8-bit pixels, Mode 7, early 3D polygons, modern landscapes) with short fact cards, and end with a mosaic of every era
collapsing back into a single dot. Build it on the cicada-motion engine and cue sheet, with an original orchestral
score that blends with chiptune voices for the game eras.

FORMAT
- 1080 x 1080, 60 fps, 128 s = 48 bars at 90 BPM (1 bar = 2.667 s); js/cues.js is the single clock for picture
  AND music; every scene starts on a bar line.

STORY / SHOT LIST (bars, from js/cues.js)
- 0–3    intro: a CRT switches on in the dark; the title assembles in pixels; PRESS START.
- 3–6    1958 oscilloscope tennis: a green dot arcing over a net.
- 6–8    1962 Spacewar!: vector starships orbit a star on a round PDP-1 screen.
- 8–11   1972 Pong on a wood-grain TV, with the Odyssey overlay.
- 11–13  1977: a cartridge drops into the Atari 2600; colour arrives.
- 13–17  the golden age of arcades: Space Invaders, Pac-Man, Donkey Kong.
- 17–19  1983: the crash — cartridges buried in a night desert.
- 19–23  revival: Tetris, the NES and a side-scroller, the Game Boy.
- 23–25  the 16-bit console wars: Mode 7 versus speed.
- 25–29  the leap into 3D: Doom, PlayStation, Nintendo 64.
- 29–33  online: PS2, Xbox, World of Warcraft, a planet connected.
- 33–37  games for everyone: Wii, smartphones, Minecraft.
- 37–42  today: an esports arena, then a breathtaking modern landscape and the numbers.
- 42–48  epilogue: every era in a mosaic, back to the single dot, THANK YOU FOR PLAYING.

MUSIC AND SOUND (js/music/)
- 90 BPM, D major (a B-minor shadow for the crash, D minor for Doom). A real CC0 orchestra (VSCO-2 CE samples:
  strings sustain/tremolo/pizzicato/spiccato, harp, flute, clarinet, horns, glockenspiel, marimba, xylophone, piano
  and percussion) plus synthesized 8-bit chip voices (pulse, triangle, noise) with a ping-pong echo.

HOW TO REBUILD
- Samples are shared with ../cicada-motion/assets (the assets link here points to it): fetch the VSCO-2 CE samples
  with ../frog-motion/tools/fetch_vsco.js.
- Score:  node js/music/index.js build/score.wav        (run inside video-games/)
- Film:   node tools/render.js --video [--jobs 14] [--resume]   -> history-of-video-games.mp4 (needs build/score.wav)
- Checks: node tools/render.js --at 12.5 | --times 1,5,20 --cols 3 --cell 480 | --clip a b
