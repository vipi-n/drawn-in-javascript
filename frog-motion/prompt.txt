PROMPT — "FROG: the life cycle of the common frog" (MOTION EDITION, v2)
(animated short, 134.4 s, 1:1 square 1080x1080, 60 fps, stereo 48 kHz original orchestral score + sound design)

This file holds (1) the full production prompt that describes the finished video, so it can be recreated, and
(2) the sequence of requests that were used to arrive at it.  v2 = a copy of the first film (../frog, untouched)
with a new sampled-orchestra score, motion-graphic accents and a layer of tiny living details in every shot.


====================================================================================================================
PART 1 — THE PROMPT
====================================================================================================================

Create a premium, top-tier motion-design nature film about the complete life cycle of the common frog
(Rana temporaria), in real scientific detail: the spring call, amplexus and external fertilisation, frogspawn
swelling and rising, the embryo from one cell through cleavage, gastrulation (yolk plug) and the neural tube to the
heartbeat and hatching, the hatchling with external gills and a sticky gland, the operculum and the spiracle, the
grazing tadpole with its horny beak and long coiled gut, a dragonfly-nymph ambush, hind legs, the metamorphosis
(thyroid hormone, left arm out through the spiracle, tail absorbed as food, wide mouth, gills to lungs, short gut),
the froglet leaving the water in the rain, its first tongue strike, growing up through summer and autumn,
hibernation in pond-bottom mud under ice, and spring — the call again, new spawn, the cycle closing.
Every step is SHOWN; text is short. Original music that follows each scene, plus real frog recordings.

FORMAT
- 1080 x 1080 square, 60 fps, 134.4 seconds = 56 bars at 100 BPM (one bar = 2.4 s), stereo 48 kHz.
- Everything is drawn by JavaScript (Node.js + canvas); no photos, no footage, no image assets.
- Watermark: X logo + "@vipinxtwt" bottom-left on every frame.

LOOK
- Luminous painterly naturalism: layered parallax depth, soft depth-of-field (blurred foreground weeds and reeds
  sliding past the lens), god rays, live water caustics on the pond floor and under the surface, drifting motes and
  bokeh, wave-broken mirror reflections, rain rings, frost, film grain and a gentle vignette.
- A signature OVER-UNDER split view (half above, half below the wavy waterline with a bright meniscus) for the
  amphibian's double life: the dawn call, the froglet climbing onto a lily pad, the finale panorama.
- Characters are clean illustrated shapes with thin dark outlines, gradient form shading, wet specular glints and a
  rim light: an olive-brown common frog with dark blotches, a dark "mask" over the eardrum, a golden eye with a
  horizontal pupil, banded legs and long webbed feet.
- ONE continuous amphibian rig morphs hatchling -> tadpole -> froglet -> adult (outlines resampled and blended), so
  the metamorphosis is a true on-screen transformation: legs bud, the front legs bulge under the skin and pop out
  (the LEFT one through the spiracle), the tail shrinks, the eyes rise and bulge, the mouth widens, the colours turn.
- A "glass" mode shows the anatomy through the skin: long coiled larval gut, internal gills, lungs, heart, thyroid.
- The embryo (Gosner stages 1-16) is shaded per pixel as a real sphere in darkfield microscope light: dark animal
  pole / pale yolk, grey crescent, cleavage furrows (2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 200 cells) cutting in from the animal pole,
  epiboly with the blastopore lip closing around the yolk plug, the keyhole neural plate whose folds zip shut;
  then the curled tailbud embryo with gill buds and a beating heart wriggles out of its capsule.

TEXT DESIGN (lives at the waterline)
- Title: "FROG" (Optima ExtraBlack) rises letter by letter out of the night pond, golden, dripping, with
  wave-broken reflections; "THE LIFE CYCLE OF THE" above it; the letters sink back as the camera dives.
- Stage headers: a gold italic numeral (Iowan Old Style) drops onto a thin wavy "surface" line with a ripple; the
  stage name (Seravek, wide tracking) surfaces letter by letter through the line; a subline floats just beneath it.
- Callouts: a ripple pin on the subject, a drawn leader that turns into an underline, the label fills from the bottom
  like a glass of water, an optional gold italic subline.
- Time badge: a bubble (DAY / WEEK / YEAR) — when time jumps it pops into droplets and a new one rises.

STORY (bars at 100 BPM)
- 0-12 s   Night pond: moon path, reeds, mist; a frog surfaces, eyes shining, calls twice; the title rises.
- 12-18    Dive into dawn over-under: the male calls with his sac, distant males answer, a plump female swims in.
- 18-24    Amplexus (callout: nuptial pads); she lays a stream of eggs, his milt shimmers: fertilised outside.
- 24-33.6  Frogspawn swells in time-lapse, other clumps around, rises to the sunny surface; zoom into one egg.
- 33.6-52.8 The embryo macro, day badge 1 -> 14; every cell division lands on a beat.
- 52.8-62.4 Hatchlings hang on the spent jelly; external gills, sticky gland; operculum grows; spiracle; swims off.
- 62.4-76.8 Tadpole school, grazing (beak + tiny teeth), glass gut, a gulp of air; the light turns murky, a
            dragonfly nymph's mask shoots out and snatches a tadpole; alarm, scatter, calm.
- 76.8-100.8 One long take: hind legs bud -> paddle -> toes -> kick; then a dark stage: thyroid glow, the left arm
            bursts out of the spiracle, the tail feeds the body, mouth widens, gills -> lungs, gut shortens, colour
            turns, and the froglet is revealed in god rays.
- 100.8-110.4 Over-under summer: the froglet climbs onto a lily pad (tail stub), rain begins, dozens of froglets
            emerge, first tongue strike.
- 110.4-117.6 On the bank: tongue strike with the eyes pushing food down; summer -> autumn time-lapse, grows up,
            leaps back into the pond.
- 117.6-122.4 Under the ice: hibernating in mud and leaves, breathing through its skin, slow heartbeat.
- 122.4-134.4 The ice cracks into spring; it swims up, calls; a female joins; new spawn glows below; the camera pulls
            back to a panorama where a loop of light runs eggs -> tadpoles -> froglet -> frog; "FROG — the cycle
            begins again" rises from the waterline; one last croak.

MOTION
- The camera never stops: spline keyframes with continuous velocity, hand-held drift, impact shakes, zoom kicks on
  every cell division, slow orbits and pushes; parallax planes from sky to lens.
- Transitions: dive / rise through the water surface (bubbles + meniscus), lens zoom-through into an egg, the macro
  shrinking back into its egg (iris), a giant tadpole silhouette whipping across the lens, bubble curtains, a
  frog-pupil iris wipe, ice cracking into a white flash.
- Springs with overshoot on text, wriggles and kicks on beats, time-lapse light flicker.

MUSIC + SOUND (all original; nothing reused from other films)
- A real orchestra, not synths: CC0 VSCO-2 Community Edition samples (violin/viola/cello sections sustain, tremolo,
  pizzicato and spiccato; solo contrabass; harp; flute, oboe, clarinet, bassoon; French horn open + muted; glockenspiel,
  marimba, xylophone; timpani hits + rolls; suspended-cymbal swells, crash, gong, bass drum; congas, log drums, guiro,
  shaker, triangle, bell tree), velocity layers + round robins, cubic re-pitching, looped sustains, humanised timing,
  orchestral seating (pan + hall send per section), voice-led chords and diatonic harmony lines.
- 100 BPM, F major (D minor for danger and the metamorphosis). Three themes: a hopping "frog theme" (C D F | A G F C |
  D F Bb | A), its answer (C Bb A G | E F G | A G F) and a lyrical "life" theme, re-orchestrated per scene: harp
  arpeggios + flute under the night stars, the orchestra answering each croak (bassoon hops, cello pizz), a timpani roll
  and cymbal swell into a soft tutti for the title; the "pond band" (pizz bass walk, bassoon staccato hops, marimba
  off-beat skank, log drums, congas, guiro "rib-bits", shaker) for the call, the tadpoles, the froglets and spring; a
  cello love song over harp for amplexus; marimba + harp clockwork for the spawn; plucked harp/glock "cell pops" on every
  division, a low clarinet for gastrulation, a rising violin line for the neural tube, timpani heartbeats; a flute /
  clarinet duet for the hatchlings; low tremolo, creeping bassoon, muted horns and a timpani roll for the nymph, an
  orchestral hit on the strike and scurrying xylophone; a slow horn theme over harp for the legs; spiccato strings,
  drums and swelling horns (theme in D minor on oboe) for the metamorphosis into a tutti F-major reveal; pizzicato
  raindrops; a pastoral flute, a wistful autumn oboe; glass tones and a slow heartbeat under the ice; singing ice into a
  bright spring tutti, theme B in horns + violins for the panorama, a final F-major cadence, a last croak, a pizz button.
- Real CC0 recordings (Joseph Sardin, BigSoundBank): single frog croaks, frog choruses, crickets, dawn birds, robin,
  blackbird, countryside, a fly, a bumblebee, pond field ambience, rain + thunder, real splashes, water drops (title drips,
  chapter numerals), bubble pops (clock); synth only for whooshes, tongue, gulp, nymph snap, ice, heartbeat thumps.
- Mix: FFT convolution hall (synthetic stereo IR, frequency-dependent decay), slow loudness rider, glue compressor,
  tilt EQ, -15 LUFS integrated (LRA ~6 LU), look-ahead limiter at -1.2 dBFS; ambience beds muffle underwater.

TINY DETAILS + MOTION GRAPHICS (v2)
- Living pond everywhere: water fleas (Daphnia) hopping with beating antennae and copepods darting on three depth
  planes; oxygen beads on elodea leaf tips that swell, let go and rise; mosquito larvae hanging under the surface;
  a pond snail gliding over the grazing stone; water striders rowing with leg dimples (and their light spots seen from
  below); whirligig beetles spinning V-ripples; damselflies patrolling the reeds; gnats glittering in low sun; fireflies
  (reflected) and bats crossing the moon at night; a cabbage-white butterfly, seed fluff, an orb web beaded with autumn
  dew; raindrop crowns and rain beading on the lily pad.
- Characters breathe: flanks swell every few seconds, the throat pumps, water beads slide down the back and drip off,
  the eye catchlight twinkles as a turning four-point star, pupils dilate at night.
- Motion graphics in the waterline language: sound-wave arcs from every call; magnifier lenses that pop on a spring,
  tethered by tangent lines (nuptial pad x20, external gill filaments with streaming blood cells x40, tadpole mouthparts
  with keratin jaws and labial tooth rows scraping x30); a turning microscope reticle, a 1 mm scale bar and an ECG trace
  for the embryo heartbeat (and a slow one under the ice); dotted trajectories for tongue strikes and the leap; golden
  hormone rings pulsing on the beat during the metamorphosis.
- Camera + finishing: beat-synced zoom kicks in every groove section, chromatic-aberration pulses on cuts and impacts,
  a light leak sweeping across every transition.
