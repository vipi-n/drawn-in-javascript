PROMPT — "THE TORTOISE AND THE HARE: a cut-paper retelling"
(animated short, 60 s, 1080x1080, 24 fps, original synthesized score)

This file holds (1) the full production prompt that describes the finished video, so it can be recreated, and
(2) the sequence of requests that were used to arrive at it.

====================================================================================================
PART 1 — THE PROMPT
====================================================================================================

Retell the real fable of the tortoise and the hare in 60 seconds, drawn frame by frame in JavaScript as a cut-paper
animation. Take only the animation style from the reference video erpSggiWfckB7Mrl.mp4 (not its balloons or any
other content; the reference is not included). The hare is fast and overconfident, naps under a tree and wakes too
late; the tortoise keeps a slow, steady pace and wins. Add an original score.

FORMAT
- 1080 x 1080, 24 fps, 60 s.

LOOK
- Flat colour paper pieces with fabric patterns, soft paper shadows, fine ink edges with a gentle line boil,
  red-pencil sketches and a paper-grain overlay on cream paper (#f3eee3).
- Sky, hills, ground, trees and the race course are all flat paper cut-outs; dusk slowly falls during the nap.
- Both animals are posed from parameters (stride cycle, neck stretch, head lift, mood, blinks).

STORY
1. Title; the hare hops in laughing and challenges the tortoise.
2. Ready, set — at 15 s the hare explodes off the start line.
3. Far ahead, he looks back and chuckles, strolls to a tree, yawns and curls up for a nap.
4. The tortoise keeps one steady, unbroken pace past the sleeping hare.
5. The hare wakes with a start and sprints, too late.
6. At 50 s the tortoise breaks the tape; calm victory.

MUSIC
- An original score synthesized sample by sample in js/music.js: 96 BPM, 4/4 (one bar = 2.5 s), so the start (15 s)
  and the finish (50 s) land on bar lines; plucks, marimba, bells, bass, reed, pad, brass, light percussion, comic
  glides and whooshes.

HOW TO REBUILD
- Live player: open index.html.
- Video: node tortoise/tools/render.js --video   -> tortoise-and-hare.mp4 + original-score.wav
- Checks: node tortoise/tools/render.js --at 12.5 | --sheet 0,60,4   (Node.js + @napi-rs/canvas + FFmpeg)

====================================================================================================
PART 2 — HOW IT WAS PROMPTED (the requests, in order)
====================================================================================================

1. "I want to make a video of the story of the tortoise and the rabbit, where the tortoise won the race. First tell
   me if you know that story."
   -> The fable retold briefly.

2. "I don't want to use this balloon and everything from the reference I gave. Use that kind of animation in my
   video — you don't need to take everything from that video, just how the animation is done. I want the real
   story. You can polish the real story. You know the real story, right?"

3. "You need to use similar animation to erpSggiWfckB7Mrl.mp4."
   -> The cut-paper film above.
