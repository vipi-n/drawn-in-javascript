PROMPT — "NINE TO SIX: one man, one ordinary day"
(animated short, 3 min 7.5 s, 1080x1080, 24 fps, original synthesized score)

This file holds (1) the full production prompt that describes the finished video, so it can be recreated, and
(2) the sequence of requests that were used to arrive at it.

====================================================================================================
PART 1 — THE PROMPT
====================================================================================================

Make a hand-drawn animated film, drawn frame by frame in JavaScript, about one ordinary day of a single young man who
works for his dream but never gets time for his own things. He wakes up thinking about the office, has nothing to
eat, grabs breakfast on the move, squeezes into a crowded metro and stands the whole way, works until lunch, eats in
twenty minutes, works until six, rides the same crowded train back, has one beer at a bar, eats something and goes
home, where he has five minutes for his own dream. Include the metro, buildings, a computer, a phone and the beer.
Paint it like the reference video gVuAJ_uw1CUtQX3t.mp4 (style only; not included), where every frame looks freshly
painted, with music that follows each situation. All movement must look natural: walking, sitting on the chair,
typing with hands on the keyboard, eating, riding the train. Give him an innocent, ordinary face.

FORMAT
- 1080 x 1080, 24 fps, 187.5 s = 75 bars at 96 BPM (one bar = 2.5 s).

LOOK
- Hand-drawn pencil line with painted washes on cream paper; the palette turns to deep indigo in the evening;
  torn-paper style wipes between scenes; short handwritten captions.

STORY (captions from js/film.js)
1. Title card: "Nine to Six".
2. The flat in the morning — "Nothing to eat."
3. The street — "Breakfast, on the move."
4. The metro platform, then a packed carriage — "Standing. The whole way."
5. Across the viaduct to the office tower; the desk and the work.
6. "Twenty minutes for lunch."
7. The afternoon — "Same tasks. More tasks."
8. The evening train — "Same train. Even more crowded."
9. The bar — "One beer. For himself."
10. The night street home; the flat — "Five minutes a night, for his own dream." End card: "Someday."

MUSIC
- An original 96 BPM, 4/4 score synthesized sample by sample in js/music.js (plucks, marimba, electric piano, bass,
  reed, pad, brass and light drums), one section per part of the day. The little sounds of the day — alarm, phone,
  train, keys, glass, pencil — are part of the music.

HOW TO REBUILD
- Live player: open index.html.
- Video: node nine-to-six/tools/render.js --video [--from S --to S]   -> nine-to-six.mp4 + original-score.wav
- Checks: --at 12.5 | --times 5,20,50 | --sheet 0,187.5,1.1 --cols 15 --cell 64
  (Node.js + @napi-rs/canvas + FFmpeg)

====================================================================================================
PART 2 — HOW IT WAS PROMPTED (the requests, in order)
====================================================================================================

1. "I want to create a video to show how a man's life works. When he wakes up, he thinks about the office and then
   eats some food. When he doesn't have food, he eats something on the way to the office. Then he books a cab or
   takes public transport, in the rush, so he doesn't even get the chance to sit. He reaches the office after one
   hour, works until lunch, goes for lunch, works again until six, then comes back on the same train, the same
   metro, in the rush. Then he goes to a bar to drink some beer, eats something and goes back home. You can take
   the animation from the previous video, but I want the animation to be very, very good. It should have all the
   things like the metro train, buildings, computer, mobile phone, drinking beer and all. He is single, working for
   his dream, struggling, and doesn't get any time to do his own things. Even if it's long — more than 2–3 minutes
   — that's fine. You can decide."
   -> First version of the film.

2. "The walk is not looking good, improve it. While working, his hands are not typing, and in the train it's not
   looking natural. Make all the activities in the video natural."

3. "The cab looks like a toy, and when people sit in the train it looks like they vanish. The office gate is not
   good, eating is not natural, inside the metro it looks like a room, the beer glass is too big, and he walks like
   he has some disease. Make it natural again."

4. "Check gVuAJ_uw1CUtQX3t.mp4 and see how the texture and animation are done: it has a painting effect, where every
   frame looks like it's getting painted, and very good music based on the screen and the situation. Do something
   like that. Also the person's face doesn't look good — make it look innocent, like a normal face."
   -> The painted look and situation-based score described in Part 1.

5. "His sitting position is not good — he is sitting below the chair, not on it. Also, while typing, his hands are
   below the keyboard."
   -> Seat and hand positions fixed: the final film.
