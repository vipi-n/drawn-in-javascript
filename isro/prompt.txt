PROMPT — "ISRO: from a bicycle in Thumba to the Moon"
(cinematic documentary trailer, 3 min 20 s, 1920x1080, 60 fps, fully synthesized orchestral score)

This file holds (1) the full production prompt that describes the finished video, so it can be recreated, and
(2) the sequence of requests that were used to arrive at it.

====================================================================================================
PART 1 — THE PROMPT
====================================================================================================

Make a cinematic video on the history of ISRO — epic and emotional, like a documentary trailer. Go all out.
Compose the soundtrack yourself, fully synthesized in code: no samples, no audio files, no VST instruments. Cut the
visuals to the music. 1920x1080, 60 fps. No downloaded images or footage — every frame generated in code. Render the
final video with the music mixed in as an MP4.

FORMAT
- 1920 x 1080, 60 fps, 200.3 s = 74 bars at 90 BPM plus a short tail; js/cues.js holds the bar-based cue points
  shared by picture and score.

LOOK
- Illustrated, realistic-leaning scenes with a moving sky, a living sea shore and trees at the launch site, natural
  motion for the bicycle and the bullock cart, realistic rockets and launch pads; strong, readable titles that do
  not shake; the ISRO logo and the Indian flag at the end. Bloom, grain and vignette for a cinematic finish.

STORY (in order, from js/shots1.js and js/shots2.js)
1963: a fishing village on the magnetic equator with no rockets and no launch pad; Thumba; rocket parts carried on a
bicycle; Vikram Sarabhai's words; the first launch -> 1969: the Indian Space Research Organisation is founded ->
Aryabhata (1975) -> SLV-3 reaches orbit (1980) -> the APPLE satellite on a bullock cart (1981) -> the first Indian in
space (1984) -> PSLV's failed first flight and the comeback -> the Moon and Mars -> mission control -> 2019: the
Vikram lander's signal is lost -> hope and the rebuild -> LVM3 launches Chandrayaan-3 (2023) -> trajectory, lunar
orbit, the descent, touchdown, the Pragyan rover -> India turns to the Sun (Aditya-L1) -> Gaganyaan -> a recap
mosaic, the title, the flag.

MUSIC
- 90 BPM orchestral-trailer score fully synthesized in js/music.js (detuned saw string sections, synth brass and
  bass, percussion hits, risers, a hall reverb), with hits and swells on the cue points: the first launch, the
  founding of ISRO, PSLV ignition, the Moon and Mars, the descent climax and the touchdown finale.

HOW TO REBUILD
- Film (builds build/score.wav first if missing; --music forces a rebuild of the score):
    node isro/tools/render.js --video [--jobs 12] [--music]      -> isro-trailer.mp4
- Contact sheets: node isro/tools/render.js --times 1,5.5,20 | --bars 4,8.5
- Score only: node isro/js/music.js out.wav            (Node.js + @napi-rs/canvas + FFmpeg)

====================================================================================================
PART 2 — HOW IT WAS PROMPTED (the requests, in order)
====================================================================================================

1. "Make a cinematic video on the history of ISRO. Make it epic and emotional, like a documentary trailer. Go all
   out. Compose the soundtrack yourself, fully synthesized in code. No samples, no audio files, no VST instruments.
   Cut the visuals to the music. 1920x1080, 60fps. No downloaded images or footage, every frame generated in code.
   Render the final video with the music mixed in as an MP4 and save it in this repo."
   -> First version of the trailer.

2. "It's good, but make it a bit more real. The launch from the sea shore looks static — a static sea, no trees,
   very basic. Make it more realistic, add the ISRO logo and, at the end, the Indian flag. The text in between is
   very thin; make it thicker. The rocket and the launch pad should be realistic, like ISRO's. The bullock cart
   carrying the rocket and the bicycle don't move naturally, and the launch and the climb into the sky don't look
   natural either. Make it a natural-looking video."
   -> Realistic shore, trees, launch pads and rockets; natural cart and bicycle motion; bolder text; logo and flag.

3. "The attached photo is the ISRO logo, not the logo you created — replace it. This is the latest launch pad.
   Make the sky real: when the rocket is in the sky, the sky doesn't move at all, so it looks like a static
   background. Also the text vibrates whenever it appears; it should not vibrate."
   -> Logo and launch pad updated, moving skies, steady titles.

4. "From 2:34 to 2:38, why are there multiple clips of landing? Fix this. In the attached screenshot, improve the
   looks and animation. When the rocket is in the sky, the sky still looks still. In the first screenshot the
   launched rocket looks just like a light. Check and fix."
   -> The duplicate landing shots removed and the in-flight shots reworked: the final 200 s trailer.
