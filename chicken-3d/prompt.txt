PROMPT — "CHICKEN 3D: Blender look-development test"
(still renders, 1080x1080 — a test made for ../chicken, not a finished film)

This file holds (1) the prompt that describes what was built, so it can be recreated, and
(2) the requests that led to it.

====================================================================================================
PART 1 — THE PROMPT
====================================================================================================

Make a quick Blender look-development test to see whether the chicken film could use realistic 3D birds: a hen and
a rooster from CC0 base models, procedurally modelled fluffy chicks, a nest with eggs and a farm yard, lit by a
farm-field HDRI and rendered as stills with EEVEE or Cycles.

SCENE
- Lighting: Poly Haven HDRI (farm_field / kiara_1_dawn) plus a warm sun; PBR ground (Poly Haven soil and plank
  textures) with displacement; Poly Haven grass and dandelion models scattered around.
- Hen and rooster: CC0 OpenGameArt models by Cestmir Dammer (with idle and eating animations).
- Chicks: metaball body and head -> voxel remesh -> subdivision, then a 3,500-strand particle down with a root-to-tip
  colour gradient, clumping and curl; eyes, beak and legs placed by ray casting; seed-based colour variation (gold
  to brown) and a "wet" parameter; subsurface scattering and sheen for the down.
- Camera: 70–90 mm lens, f/2.8–4.5 depth of field, low angle; AgX tone mapping.

HOW TO RUN (assets/ must hold the CC0 downloads; see assets/oga/*/license.txt)
- blender -b -P tools/lookdev.py -- [eevee|cycles] /tmp/ck/lookdev.png
- blender -b -P tools/look_chick.py -- [eevee|cycles] /tmp/ck/look_chick.png
- blender -b -P tools/look_shots.py -- [yard|nest] /tmp/ck/shot.png [cycles|eevee]
- tools/lib3d.py holds the shared scene, material and model helpers.

====================================================================================================
PART 2 — HOW IT WAS PROMPTED (the requests, in order, from the chicken film's chat)
====================================================================================================

   animation. The chicks, hen and chicken are not looking realistic. Do you want to use Blender or something?"
   -> This Blender test was made.
